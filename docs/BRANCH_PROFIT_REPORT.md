# GRIDMIND branch profitability report (GR5-64)

Generated by `luau tools/branch_report.luau` (`tools/branch_report.sh`). Deterministic: seeds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 90 game minutes per window, stub AI, x1 game speed.

## Method

- Every arm replays the scripted pacing player (`config/pacing.luau`) on its seed from game second 0 to the **fork**: `t2` = minute 15 (Engine Mk2 bought, the earliest Salvage can open), `t4` = minute 70 (logistics hub standing; Raiding, Refining and Salvage are all openable on the same assets).
- Both arms of a pair receive the same credit grant at the fork (the largest branch unlock they are compared with, 5000 working capital, and a freighter for the logistics controls). The branch arm opens its branch (paying the unlock) and briefs its agent with the directive shown; the control keeps the credits. The shop is frozen in both arms afterwards, Compute is topped up by the pacing player in both.
- Controls: `hmc` = Miners sell at HMC (the pacing player). `orbis` / `ship` = Miners store ore in the warehouse (saved skill `gm-store`, the stub Miner never stores) on iridium + crystal, one Logistician and one freighter move it with the directive "Sell at Orbis." / "Ship everything you can to the markets." Refining is compared with these because it can only run on stored ore and sell through a Logistician, so the logistics uplift is not credited to the refinery.
- **Net** = change in net worth over the window of the branch arm minus that of its control. Net worth = credits + stock at its best unlocked sale price (ore at the HMC rate) + freighter and ship loads + Compute at pack price, so unlock, operating, repair, Compute, input opportunity cost (ore not sold) and losses are all inside it; the unlock is paid before the window and is the payback target. **Net cash** is the same on credits only (nothing marked to market). **Gross** = credits the branch agent itself earned (Scavenger / Privateer sales and loot); Refining has no gross of its own, its goods are sold by the Logistician, so its revenue is shown as `refined sales`.
- Payback = first game minute from which the marginal net worth stays at or above the unlock cost to the end of the window; `~N (extrap.)` = unlock / marginal rate when not reached in the window; `never` = marginal rate is not positive.
- Missions are not claimed in the window (claiming pays V1 3,000 cr, V2 a blueprint, V3 5,000 cr: excluded from every figure); status shows what the directive achieved.
- **Assumptions and unreachable buyers.** Stub AI everywhere (no real LLM). Mark-to-market valuation: unsold stock counts at its best unlocked sale price, so a stalled stock inflates Net without being cash (compare Net with Net cash). Orbis buys neither Catalyst nor Processor and the stub Logistician stalls on a "Sell at Orbis." directive when it holds one, so the `Sold at Orbis` Refining arms leave goods stranded: their Net is **not realizable** and the `Shipped` arms are the Refining result. Data cores sell only at Meridian (`can_meridian`). One freighter is the logistics bottleneck. Missions are not claimed.
